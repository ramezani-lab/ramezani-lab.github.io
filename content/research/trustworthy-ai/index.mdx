Machine learning systems deployed on social platforms are adversarial by
nature: the content they judge is produced by people who know they are being
judged. We work on detection under that pressure — multi-modal fake news
detection across text and images, audio-visual deepfake detection that
generalises to unseen domains, and labelling news as early in its propagation as
accuracy allows.

A second thread runs through all of it: a model should know what it does not
know. We study epistemic uncertainty in prediction, so that a system can abstain
instead of asserting.
