title: "Artificial Intelligence Applications in Health Insurances: A Scoping Review"
abstract: >-
  This scoping review examines literature published between 2000 and 2024 on
  the application of artificial intelligence in health insurance, searching
  PubMed, Scopus and Web of Science. AI presents numerous opportunities in
  health insurance, including agenda setting, financial management, fraud
  detection, monitoring, diagnostics, risk management and value creation,
  alongside ethical challenges that must be addressed for effective
  implementation. Policies should prioritise the protection of personal health
  data, address ethical concerns, and ensure robust data privacy and security.
