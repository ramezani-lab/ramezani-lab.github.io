title: "Applications of Artificial Intelligence and the Challenges in Health Technology Assessment: A Scoping Review and Framework with a Focus on Economic Dimensions"
abstract: >-
  Health Technology Assessment (HTA) is a crucial tool for evaluating health
  technologies and providing evidence-based guidance for their adoption. This
  study explores the opportunities and challenges of using artificial
  intelligence in HTA, with a specific focus on economic dimensions, following
  Arksey and O'Malley's scoping review framework over PubMed, Scopus and Web
  of Science. AI enhances HTA outcomes by driving methodological advancement,
  improving utility and enabling comprehensive assessment through robust data
  systems; ethical considerations such as bias, transparency and
  accountability require deliberate planning and policymaking for responsible
  integration.
