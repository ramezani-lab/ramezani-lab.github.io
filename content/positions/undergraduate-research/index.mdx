Undergraduate researchers join an ongoing project alongside a graduate student.
The commitment is a real one — roughly ten hours a week for at least two terms —
and the work is expected to end in something concrete: a benchmark, a dataset,
an implementation, or a section of a paper.

Strong programming ability matters more than coursework.
