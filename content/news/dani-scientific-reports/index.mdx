DANI infers a social network from observed cascades while preserving the
properties that make the recovered graph useful — modular structure, degree
distribution, density and clustering coefficients — in linear time.

The paper is open access, and the implementation is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/AryanAhadinia/DANI).
